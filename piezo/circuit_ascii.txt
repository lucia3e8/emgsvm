    Key Components:
    - Piezo: ~50mV signal source with 1nF capacitance
    - Input Buffer: High-Z unity gain buffer
    - AC Coupling: 1µF cap with 1MΩ DC restoration
    - Gain Stage: Non-inverting amp with gain of 10
    - Output: Clamped to 0-3.3V range
